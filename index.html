Who we are

Wound care that starts with the person in the bed.

Lavida Heal is a wound care group serving skilled nursing facilities across Colorado. We bring evidence-based wound management directly to the bedside — so residents get the care they need without leaving the comfort of home, and your team gets a clinical partner they can reach.

  • Physicians, surgeons & advanced-practice practitioners with specialized wound-care training
  • On-site care that helps residents avoid transport to the hospital and doctor's office
  • Findings and outcomes shared with your team the same day

Escalation & surgical expertise

Not every wound requires transfer. Not every wound can stay in place. Our role is helping facilities recognize the difference — early.

Flaps, grafts, dehiscence, exposed hardware, and challenging postoperative wounds — managed by a clinical team with surgical expertise, with prompt escalation when operative intervention is needed.

Where we serve

Based in Denver, at bedsides across Colorado.

Our clinicians round from Denver out to five coverage zones — the metro, the Northeast plains, the Western Slope, the Arkansas Valley and the Royal Gorge — on a scheduled weekly rhythm, with a clinician reachable in between. Not in a zone yet? Coverage grows as facilities join.
