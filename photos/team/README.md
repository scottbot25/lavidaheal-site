# Team headshots

Drop real provider headshots in this folder with these **exact filenames** and they
will automatically appear on the website once the team section is restored. The filename is
`firstname-lastname.jpg`, lower-case, one per clinician, matching the card in `_team-section.html`.

**Photo tips:** square-ish crop (the card crops to ~1:1), shoulders-up, soft even
lighting, plain or softly blurred background, warm smile. Aim for 800×800px or larger.
JPG format. Consistent style across all five reads as one team.
